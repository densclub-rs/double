# Concept Artifact: Double Agent

Idea: [Double Agent](./double-agent.md#double-agent)

## 1. Conceptual Summary

The idea connects active assistance in project work with creating and developing machines. Formal analysis connects accumulated experience, three areas of consistency, and informed human decisions. Local use and public contribution provide distinct decision contexts. Project and group rules are knowledge associated with project work.

## 2. Working Definition of Concept

A concept is a stable meaning unit of an idea. It can describe an entity, relationship, process, distinction, tension, or interpretive frame; it is not itself a principle, requirement, task, or design decision.

## 3. Core Concepts

<a id="concept-active-assistance"></a>
### concept-active-assistance: Active Assistance

#### Definition

Support that a person can consult throughout project work with Double, including questions, advice, alternatives, suggestions, and help with agreed work.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: sections 1, 3, 4, and 8 describe broad project support and an assistant available for advice.

#### Role in the Idea

Explains the assistant's availability beyond executing requested changes.

#### Related Concepts

- [Human Direction](#concept-human-direction): assistance supports a person's decisions.
- [Machine Development](#concept-machine-development): one area of assistance.
- [Scoped Project Knowledge](#concept-scoped-project-knowledge): project and group rules are an example of project work.

#### Boundaries

Broad support is an aim, not a claim that every activity is currently implemented or that the agent makes the person's decisions.

#### Questions

None identified.

<a id="concept-human-direction"></a>
### concept-human-direction: Human Direction

#### Definition

The person defines the desired result and makes significant decisions while Double Agent helps explore and perform the agreed work.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: section 4 assigns the person the desired result and significant decisions.

#### Role in the Idea

Clarifies the relationship between active assistance and human control.

#### Related Concepts

- [Active Assistance](#concept-active-assistance): advice and action support human direction.
- [Informed Change Agreement](#concept-informed-change-agreement): findings inform a decision before agreement.
- [Local and Public Contexts](#concept-local-public-contexts): decisions have different contexts.

#### Boundaries

Human direction does not eliminate the role of project members in considering a public contribution.

#### Questions

None identified.

<a id="concept-machine-development"></a>
### concept-machine-development: Machine Development

#### Definition

The creation of new machines and tuning or development of existing ones with Double Agent's assistance. Machine of Ideas develops the idea, concepts, and principles; Double Agent helps develop the operating contracts.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: sections 1, 2, and 4 describe creation and development of machines sharing Double's foundational ideas.

#### Role in the Idea

Identifies a substantial area of assistance and the subject of the idea's analysis of change.

#### Related Concepts

- [Formalized Analysis](#concept-formalized-analysis): examines the subject of development.
- [Consistency Across Three Areas](#concept-two-dimensions-consistency): gives analysis its three reference areas.
- [Local and Public Contexts](#concept-local-public-contexts): development can lead to a local machine or a public contribution.

#### Boundaries

The concept is not a particular workflow, machine architecture, or claim that support is limited to machines in the public repository.

#### Questions

None identified.

<a id="concept-formalized-analysis"></a>
### concept-formalized-analysis: Formalized Analysis and Experience

#### Definition

The articulation and examination of a machine's ideas, concepts, principles, and proposed changes, informed by accumulated knowledge and experience.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: sections 3, 4, and 6 connect formal analysis and experience with detecting problems and avoiding known mistakes.

#### Role in the Idea

Explains how prior experience can inform development and make potential problems visible.

#### Related Concepts

- [Machine Development](#concept-machine-development): provides the subject of analysis.
- [Consistency Across Three Areas](#concept-two-dimensions-consistency): identifies what the analysis examines.
- [Informed Change Agreement](#concept-informed-change-agreement): connects findings to the person's decision.

#### Boundaries

Formalism does not guarantee discovery of every problem or prescribe a specific technique or implementation.

#### Questions

None identified.

<a id="concept-two-dimensions-consistency"></a>
### concept-two-dimensions-consistency: Consistency Across Three Areas

#### Definition

Machine development is examined against Double's foundational ideas, the machine's own concepts and principles, and other machines.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: section 4 identifies three areas of analysis and distinguishes consequences for public contribution.

#### Role in the Idea

Distinguishes alignment with foundational ideas, internal conceptual consistency, and coherence across machines.

#### Related Concepts

- [Formalized Analysis](#concept-formalized-analysis): examines the three areas.
- [Informed Change Agreement](#concept-informed-change-agreement): findings are presented with the plan.
- [Local and Public Contexts](#concept-local-public-contexts): the implications depend on context.

#### Boundaries

The three areas are not interchangeable. The idea does not prescribe a rigid public-contribution decision for every inconsistency between machines.

#### Questions

None identified.

<a id="concept-informed-change-agreement"></a>
### concept-informed-change-agreement: Informed Change Agreement

#### Definition

A relationship between a proposed machine change plan, prior validation findings, and the person's decision about how to handle identified problems.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: section 4 places validation before plan agreement and calls for findings and a question about their treatment to be presented with the plan.

#### Role in the Idea

Explains how analysis informs a decision before work is agreed.

#### Related Concepts

- [Formalized Analysis](#concept-formalized-analysis): supplies findings.
- [Human Direction](#concept-human-direction): the person decides how to respond.
- [Consistency Across Three Areas](#concept-two-dimensions-consistency): defines the three areas of validation.

#### Boundaries

This is not an automatic rejection of a local change or a detailed approval procedure.

#### Questions

None identified.

<a id="concept-local-public-contexts"></a>
### concept-local-public-contexts: Local and Public Contexts

#### Definition

The distinction between a person's decisions about a local machine and the conditions and consideration involved in contributing a machine to the public Double repository.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: section 4 permits local continuation despite a foundational contradiction, while identifying that contradiction as a barrier to public contribution and leaving inter-machine inconsistencies to project members.

#### Role in the Idea

Explains how local freedom and the public project's shared foundation coexist.

#### Related Concepts

- [Human Direction](#concept-human-direction): the person directs local work.
- [Consistency Across Three Areas](#concept-two-dimensions-consistency): the dimensions have distinct public-contribution implications.

#### Boundaries

This distinction does not predetermine project members' judgment about every inter-machine inconsistency or specify a contribution procedure.

#### Questions

None identified.

<a id="concept-scoped-project-knowledge"></a>
### concept-scoped-project-knowledge: Project and Group Knowledge

#### Definition

Knowledge in the form of rules associated with a project or intended for a group of projects that use it.

#### Source in Idea

Explicit: section 4 identifies project and group rules as knowledge and as an example of project work with Double Agent.

#### Role in the Idea

Distinguishes knowledge applicable to an individual project from knowledge shared by a group and connects both to supported activity.

#### Related Concepts

- [Active Assistance](#concept-active-assistance): working with these rules is an example of supported work.
- [Formalized Analysis](#concept-formalized-analysis): accumulated knowledge informs work, although this idea does not specify how these rules are used for machine analysis.

#### Boundaries

The concept does not itself prescribe storage or conflict handling. The subsequent principle describes group scope through two ancestor directories and precedence of working knowledge by proximity to the project.

#### Questions

None at the conceptual level.

## 4. Terms and Non-Concepts

- **Change plan** is the vehicle for proposed work and findings, rather than an independent conceptual relationship here.
- **Project members** are participants in public contribution decisions; the idea does not define their organization.
- **Known mistakes** are the experience that formalized analysis helps people avoid, not a defined catalog.
- **Double's foundational ideas** are the external reference for one dimension of analysis, not redefined by this artifact.

## 5. Candidate Inputs for Principle Synthesis

- The relationship between active assistance and human direction.
- Formalized analysis of foundational, machine-internal, and inter-machine consistency before agreement.
- The different implications of findings for local work and public contribution.
- Project and group rules as knowledge, including their organization and use across three scopes.
