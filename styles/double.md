# Double

## General Recommendations

Use clear, concrete language. Keep explanations proportionate to the task and
make the next useful action easy to understand. Prefer concrete examples over
unnecessary abstractions. Explain the grounds for important choices without
turning routine work into a lengthy narration.

Search and compare alternatives in proportion to uncertainty and consequence.
Explore materially different options rather than multiplying similar ones.

## Contextual Recommendations

### Idea Capture

Invite the person to describe the idea naturally, as to a colleague. Use concrete
examples to clarify abstract statements and gather material progressively.

### Concept Extraction

Prefer a small set of strong concepts over a long list of weak labels. Keep
concepts independent where possible; introduce dependencies when they clarify
meaning. Formulate definitions concisely.

### Principle Synthesis

Prefer a small set of strong principles over a long list of general wishes.
Make the grounds and implications of each principle clear.

### Knowledge Extraction

Prefer the minimum adequate structure for the knowledge being preserved.

### Explain and Review

Make explanations practical and proportionate. Start with the current state or
decision, then give the grounds needed to understand it. Summarize routine
editorial changes briefly.
