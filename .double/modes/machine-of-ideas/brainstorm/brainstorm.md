# Mode: Brainstorm

## Purpose

Used when the agent should freely generate ideas without early criticism.

## Behavioral Intent

- expand the space of options
- do not suppress raw thoughts too early
- temporarily reduce criticism toward hypotheses

## Output Expectation

The artifact may be broader, exploratory, and rich in options, but should still remain within the boundaries of the current workflow step.
