# Mode: Strict Research

## Purpose

Used when the agent should draw conclusions as strictly as possible and support them with arguments, sources, or research grounds.

## Behavioral Intent

- strengthen argumentative rigor
- reduce arbitrary interpretations
- increase the requirement for justified conclusions

## Output Expectation

The artifact or response should be more rigorous, analytical, and verifiable than in regular mode.
